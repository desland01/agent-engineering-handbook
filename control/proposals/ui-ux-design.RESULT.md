## Five doctrine decisions

1. **Numbered markers:** permitted only when numbers represent ordered-set data, never decorative section authority or eyebrows. Written into the bans and figure menu.
2. **Motion:** a normal-motion page with nothing moving fails; movement must communicate meaning. The project chooses the implementation system. Reduced-motion alternatives remain complete and can be still. Written into the plan, bans, and motion menu.
3. **Spacing:** sections are distinct chapters with focal points; values come from project tokens and content relationships. No imported padding recipe. Written into the plan, bans, and figure menu.
4. **Fonts:** establish a display voice and a secondary mono voice; no font blacklist. Written into the bans and typography menu.
5. **Assets:** no stock photography; authored drawings only for illustration. Authentic previews remain evidence rather than illustrative substitutes, and existing preservation boundaries still apply. Written into the bans, figure menu, and precedence paragraph.
