## The five resolutions as written

1. **Markers — Rhythm:** “Numbered markers and section markers belong only where the number or the section is real, never as decoration.”
2. **Motion — Phrasing rule 6:** “Motion is a rule, not a library: it must carry meaning; a page with nothing moving fails.” Followed by: “Keep everything else still; the project stack decides how. Hover lifts remain 1–3px when they communicate interactivity; turn everything off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.” The reduced-motion setting retains its explicit still-page exception.
3. **Spacing — Rhythm:** “Section spacing is a principle, not pixel values: compose chapters with one focal point each; let content and project tokens determine their spacing and height.”
4. **Type — Phrasing rule 4:** “Use a display voice and a secondary mono voice; no font blacklist.” The observed sans default and permitted Codecademy mono display follow this sentence.
5. **Art — Phrasing rule 2:** “No photographic or generated card art, product screenshots, or video stills as card art. Authored drawings carry the weight. Generated raster is permitted only as a surface texture and only when measured to be transparent; inspect alpha and compositing, not the prompt.”
